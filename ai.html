AI adoption & enablement consulting

AI adoption with clear direction and practical support.

Your teams may already be experimenting with AI, but priorities, tool choices and ownership are unclear. Or you may need someone to establish an adoption programme and take it through to delivery. I help businesses make those decisions and put them into practice.

Discuss your AI priorities ↗

A standalone service

A practical AI adoption programme.

The starting point is your business: where people spend time, which processes create friction and what would make a useful difference. From there, we agree priorities, ownership and how progress will be measured.

I can lead adoption across the organisation or take on a focused piece of work, from reviewing tools to building an initial automation.

Choose the right use cases

Build and prioritise a backlog around business value, feasibility and risk, and identify overlapping or low-value ideas before they absorb time.

Give teams clear guidance

Define practical rules for AI use, data handling, security, access and review, working within your existing requirements.

Build ownership across the business

Establish an AI champion network, with clear responsibilities and regular working sessions to share learning and support adoption.

Make considered tooling decisions

Review existing tools and usage, identify duplication and guide investment around what teams need.

Understand adoption and value

Track usage and spend, identify where teams need support and agree measures for priority use cases.

Keep decisions moving

Give executive sponsors a clear view of progress, risks and decisions, while working with functional leaders to resolve blockers.

I can establish and lead the programme alongside your existing team, or take on a defined piece of work such as a tooling review or initial automation. We agree the scope around your priorities and capacity.

Recent experience

Company-wide AI adoption.

Established a company-wide AI adoption programme within a technology business, including executive sponsorship, governance, enterprise tooling and an internal champion network. An intake and triage process generated more than 20 validated use cases within weeks.

I lead prioritisation and work with functional leaders and champions to support adoption. The remit also includes AI policy, access controls, tooling investment and ongoing spend management, with reporting that helps sponsors decide where to focus.

This is programme leadership backed by practical implementation. Alongside the adoption work, I build tools and automations that teams can use.

How I use AI in delivery

Senior expertise with more hands-on capacity.

AI also expands the work I can take on for clients. I use it to support research, analysis, writing, software development and automation, bringing hands-on capacity alongside senior Product Delivery experience.
