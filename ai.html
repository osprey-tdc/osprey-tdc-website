How we use AI

AI does the work. Experience sets the standard.

We use AI to research, analyse, write, build and automate. That expands what one experienced consultant can deliver, without asking a client to fund a large support team.

But AI is never left to work blindly. Our Product Delivery and technology experience is what makes the approach effective. We give AI specific instructions, provide the right context, check assumptions and review outputs before they're used. It's much like directing a junior member of a delivery or operations team: brief properly, review the work, challenge what's wrong and take responsibility for the result.

We decide what needs human judgement, what can safely be automated and when something needs further checks or client approval. Accountability stays with us.

A standalone service

AI enablement across your business.

We can also lead a structured AI adoption programme. That means finding where AI will genuinely help, setting sensible rules and giving people the confidence and tools to use it well.

  • Opportunities: use-case discovery, intake and prioritisation across teams.
  • Governance: usage policies, data safeguards, access controls and approval points.
  • Adoption: champion networks, guidance and practical training.
  • Delivery: initial automations, tools and ways to measure usage and cost.

We've established a company-wide AI programme from the ground up, including governance, enterprise tooling, an internal champion network and a pipeline of more than 20 use cases.

Practical arrangements

Your tools. Clear controls.

Clients provide the agreed AI accounts and tools, with appropriate access and permissions. We work within the client's security and data-handling requirements.

All work is directed and reviewed by Osprey TDC. Automated activity is limited to what has been agreed, with human checks and approvals where needed. Engagements are based on an agreed day rate and scope.
