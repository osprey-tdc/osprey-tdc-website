How an engagement works

From priorities to implementation.

I start with your growth plans, priorities and current delivery capability. We agree where support will make the most difference and a scope that fits: a focused review with implementation, a defined improvement project or ongoing fractional leadership.

I stay involved in putting the changes into practice, whether that means a clearer delivery plan, a revised operating model or more useful reporting. Scope and support are agreed upfront.
